Seongkwan Yang is a Principal Engineer at Samsung Electronics, Audio Group, with 12 years of experience in acoustic transducer and speaker design. He holds a B.S. from Hanyang University and an M.S. from POSTECH in Mechanical Engineering. In Fall 2026, he will begin his PhD at the University of Texas at Austin under Professor Michael Haberman. His research interests include Acoustic Metamaterials, Micro Acoustic Transducers, Computational Acoustics, and MEMS Sensor Design.
